# Visual references

`blood-relay-visual-direction.webp` is the validated gameplay/art-direction mockup for Blood Relay.

## Platforms

The selected platform direction is **A** from the platform exploration: dark industrial metal, readable modular plates, cyan light accents and very sparse red wear marks.

The platform look remains **procedural** rather than being replaced by a fixed production sprite sheet. This is part of the visual identity of the prototype and keeps the arena flexible while preserving the industrial pixel-art feel.

Floating platforms are built around a strict visual center:

- equal left and right caps;
- an odd number of complete repeatable center modules;
- cyan accents mirrored around the center;
- one centered red wear detail;
- platform widths adjusted to remain compatible with this symmetric construction.

The arena floor remains a continuous repeatable procedural surface and is not constrained by the floating-platform symmetry rule.
